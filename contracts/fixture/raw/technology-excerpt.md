<summary><b>Q2 2026 (Jun 30, 2026) — Full Summary</b></summary>

**Headline results:**
Shopify delivered another blowout quarter with revenue of $3.58 billion, up 33.7% year over year, surpassing consensus estimates by 4.4%. Adjusted EPS of $0.42 beat the Street by 7.7%, increasing 20% year over year. This marked the fifth consecutive quarter of GMV growth above 30%, as GMV reached $115.57 billion (+31.6% YoY). All key metrics — GMV, revenue, gross profit, and free cash flow — grew above 30%.

**Segment performance:**
Subscription Solutions revenue was $802 million, up 22% YoY, driven by continued merchant net additions across standard and Plus plans. Merchant Solutions revenue surged 37.4% to $2.78 billion, powered by higher GMV and expanding Shopify Payments penetration, which reached 68% of global GMV (up 3 percentage points YoY). Merchant Solutions now represents approximately 78% of total revenue.

**Sector-specific metrics:**
GMV of $115.57 billion was the fifth consecutive quarter above the 30% growth threshold, underscoring sustained merchant activity. Shopify Payments processed an ever-greater share of GMV, reflecting deepening platform monetization. MRR data was not separately disclosed this quarter, but the subscription revenue trajectory and Plus mix suggest continued healthy merchant unit economics.

**Margin & profitability:**
Subscription Solutions gross margin was 79.7%, remaining robust despite rising AI inference costs from Sidekick usage. Merchant Solutions gross margin was 38.4%, reflecting the lower-margin payments and logistics mix. Blended operating margin expanded to 17.4%, a meaningful improvement. Free cash flow rose to $654 million from $422 million a year ago, with FCF margin expanding to 18% from 16%. R&D spending continues to be elevated given AI investment.

**Balance sheet & capital:**
The $2 billion share repurchase program announced in Q4 2025 is expected to be ongoing. Shopify continues to generate significant cash, with trailing 12-month free cash flow comfortably above $2 billion.

**Management commentary:**
CEO Tobi Lutke and CFO Jeff Hoffmeister highlighted the broad-based nature of growth across geographies, merchant sizes, and sales channels. Management emphasized the compounding effect of Shopify Payments adoption and AI-driven merchant productivity tools. The Sidekick AI assistant saw daily active merchants increase 3.6x and daily sessions rise 4.8x, indicating rapid AI adoption.

**Guidance:**
For Q3 2026, management guided to low-thirties percentage revenue growth year over year, expected to be broad-based across geographies and channels. Free cash flow margin guidance was set at high-teens to low-twenties percentage.

**Market reaction:**
Shares surged approximately 18% following the earnings release, reflecting relief and enthusiasm over the sustained growth trajectory and expanding profitability. The stock reaction was among the strongest post-earnings moves in recent quarters.

**Analyst/notable quotes:**
Analysts broadly praised the results as demonstrating Shopify's unique positioning at the intersection of commerce infrastructure and AI. Consensus rating remains "Buy" with an average price target around $147, though several analysts raised targets post-earnings.
