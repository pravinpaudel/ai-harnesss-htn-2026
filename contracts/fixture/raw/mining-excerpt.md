## 1. Executive Summary

- **Sector view:** Bullish — The Canadian mining sector is experiencing a generational tailwind from record commodity prices. Gold has surged from ~$2,000/oz in mid-2024 to over $4,000/oz, while copper has climbed from $4.18/lb to $6.05/lb. **Thesis:** Ivanhoe Mines operates three world-class assets in the DRC and South Africa at various stages of development. Kamoa-Kakula is one of the world's highest-grade copper mines, but production has been in a transitional trough — declining from a record 133,120t/quarter (Q1 2025) to ~62,000t (Q1-Q2 2026) as high-grade surface stockpiles were depleted and the operation transitions to underground-only feed. The on-site 500,000 tpa copper smelter (Africa's largest) produced its first anodes Dec 29, 2025, and is transforming the cost structure by reducing logistics costs from $0.70/lb to $0.22/lb and generating sulphuric acid by-product revenue. Kipushi zinc has been an exceptional ramp-up story: from first production of 18,946t (Q3 2024) to a record 70,177t (Q2 2026). Platreef Phase 1 achieved first concentrate Nov 2025 but commercial production has been delayed to Q4 2026. NOTE: IVN uses equity-method accounting for Kamoa-Kakula — consolidated revenue is mostly Kipushi only; KK revenue ($880M in Q2 2026) flows through "share of profit from joint venture."

**Catalysts:**
- Kamoa-Kakula production recovery: targeting 290-310Kt for 2026, 380-420Kt for 2027, 500Kt+ from 2028
- Smelter ramp to full 500,000 tpa capacity (currently at 60%)
- Platreef commercial production (Q4 2026)
- Western Forelands (Makoko) updated MRE expected Sep 2026
- Kipushi tracking upper end of 240-290Kt zinc guidance

**Risks:**
- DRC sovereign risk (tax settlements, miners' strikes, regulatory uncertainty)
- Kamoa-Kakula production trough — Q2 2026 at 64,328t anode is well below 2028 target of 500Kt+
- Elevated net debt ($1.3B pro-rata) with 2.4x leverage; $750M Senior Notes due 2030
- Platreef ramp-up risk (commercial production delayed twice)
- Equity-method accounting makes IVN financial statements difficult to interpret
