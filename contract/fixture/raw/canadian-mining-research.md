## 1. Executive Summary

- **Sector view:** Bullish — The Canadian mining sector is experiencing a generational tailwind from record commodity prices. Gold has surged from ~$2,000/oz in mid-2024 to over $4,000/oz, while copper has climbed from $4.18/lb to $6.05/lb. Five of six companies have seen revenue roughly double or more over the trailing 8-quarter period.

- **Top opportunities:** AEM — best-in-class operational consistency, beat EPS 7 of 8 quarters, balance sheet swung from $632M net debt to $3,158M net cash, AISC discipline at $1,225-$1,350/oz with gold above $4,000; TECK.B — beat EPS all 8 quarters, copper transformation complete post-coal sale, Anglo American merger creates a top-5 global copper producer, gross margin expanded from 16.7% to 46.3%; WPM — streaming model delivering 87% gross margins, $4.3B Antamina deal is the largest streaming transaction in history, production growth to 1.2M GEOs by 2030.

- **Key catalysts this period:** ABX North American IPO evaluation (targeted year-end 2026), TECK.B Anglo American merger remaining regulatory approvals, FM hedge expiration (all contracts expired June 30, 2026 — H2 full spot exposure), IVN Kamoa-Kakula smelter ramp to full capacity, WPM Antamina stream contribution ramping, AEM Hope Bay feasibility study mid-2026.
