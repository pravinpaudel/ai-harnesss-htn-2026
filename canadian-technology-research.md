## 1. Executive Summary
- **Sector view:** Constructive — Canadian technology is a diverse group spanning e-commerce platforms, serial acquirers, IT services, enterprise software, and supply chain SaaS. The sector is benefiting from three secular tailwinds: (1) AI adoption across enterprise workflows, (2) tariff/trade complexity driving demand for compliance and supply chain tools, and (3) cloud migration momentum. Growth profiles range from 2% (OTEX) to 34% (SHOP), with profitability improving across the group. Valuations are mixed — SHOP trades at premium multiples justified by 30%+ growth, while OTEX and GIB.A offer value with single-digit P/E ratios.
- **Top opportunities:** SHOP — sustained 30%+ revenue growth at scale, GMV crossed $100B quarterly, Payments penetration expanding (58% → 68%), $2B buyback signals capital return maturity | DSG — beat or met estimates all 8 quarters, gross margin expanded 400bps to 78%, zero debt with $401M cash, tariff complexity is a structural demand tailwind | KXS — SaaS growth re-accelerated from 14% to 20%+, Maestro AI at 10% adoption, Rule of 40 achieved (16% growth + 26% EBITDA margin)

- **Key catalysts this period:** SHOP Q3 2026 earnings (late Oct), CSU Q3 2026 earnings (Nov), GIB.A Q4 FY2026 earnings (Nov), OTEX Q1 FY2027 earnings (Oct/Nov), DSG Q3 FY2027 earnings (Dec), KXS Q3 2026 earnings (Nov), BoC rate decisions (impact on tech valuations)
